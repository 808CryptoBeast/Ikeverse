The Journey

IKEVERSE ROADMAP

Building a living knowledge graph — from foundation research to a global platform connecting ancestral wisdom with modern understanding.

2023 – 2024
PHASE 1 · FOUNDATION

Research, theoretical framework, and initial documentation of ancestral knowledge systems.

  • 📚 Historical and archaeological research compilation across 55+ cultures
  • 🔍 Preliminary analysis of connections across civilizations
  • 🧠 Theoretical framework development — the living knowledge graph concept
  • 📊 Project roadmap, white paper first draft, and cultures.json schema
  • 🌐 Pikoverse ecosystem architecture designed
COMPLETE ✓
2024 – 2025
PHASE 2 · CODIFICATION

Building the digital infrastructure — cultures.json, the Cosmic Weave 3D globe, and the Ikeverse data architecture.

  • 🌐 Cosmic Weave interactive 3D globe launched — 55 cultures, 113 connections
  • ⭐ Real celestial star map — 65 named stars with 8 cultural naming traditions
  • 🎯 Guided cultural tour — 5-culture stop experience
  • 🔗 All 55 cultures fully documented — story, research papers, reading links
  • 🛡️ Admin console — full CRUD for cultures, links, and export
  • 📱 Mobile-optimized with bottom-sheet culture cards and bottom nav dock
COMPLETE ✓
2025 – 2026
PHASE 3 · ACTIVATION

Bringing the knowledge graph to a wider audience — Next.js portal, culture profiles, and community features.

  • 🚀 Pikoverse.xyz portal — Next.js landing page with embedded globe
  • 📄 Culture cards — 55 filterable cards pulling live from cultures.json
  • 🗺️ Dedicated /explore route — full-page immersive globe experience
  • 🔍 Global search — fuzzy search by name, region, era, and theme
  • ⭐ Star culture toggle — switch star names between 8 naming traditions
  • 📊 Compare mode — side-by-side civilizational comparison panel
  • 🌍 Expanded network — 113 links, zero isolated cultures, 4.1 avg connections
IN PROGRESS · 72%
2026 – 2027
PHASE 4 · GALACTIC WEAVING

Deep personalization, community contributions, and multi-modal knowledge access.

  • 👤 User accounts — save favourite cultures, create custom knowledge paths
  • 📝 Community contributions — verified cultural scholars can add content
  • 🎮 Guided learning journeys — curated 8-culture thematic tours with quizzes
  • 🌐 Multilingual support — content in indigenous languages alongside English
  • 📡 API — open access to the knowledge graph for researchers and educators
  • 📱 Native mobile apps — iOS and Android with offline culture packs
AWAITING PHASE 3
2027 – beyond
PHASE 5 · COSMIC SYNTHESIS

The knowledge graph becomes a living global institution — interoperable, self-sustaining, and community-governed.

  • 🏛️ 12+ indigenous cosmologies fully documented and interoperable
  • 🤝 Partnerships with cultural institutions, universities, and elder councils
  • 🔗 Blockchain-secured oral history archive — immutable community records
  • 🧬 AI-assisted pattern recognition across knowledge traditions
  • 🌍 Education integration — K–12 and university curriculum modules
  • 🎓 Certified cultural practitioner pathways — community recognition
  • ⚡ Real-time collaborative knowledge building — decentralized

FEATURES IN DEVELOPMENT

Planned additions to the Cosmic Weave globe and the broader Ikeverse platform.

Stellarium Mode

Full planetarium integration — real-time star positions, constellation overlays from multiple cultures, and precession simulation across centuries.

🗂️
Lazy Loading

Split cultures.json into a 22KB lean metadata file loaded instantly, with rich detail (290KB) fetched per-culture on demand — cutting initial load by 42%.

🔖
User Bookmarks

Save favourite cultures, annotate connections, and build personal knowledge paths stored in Supabase user profiles.

🌙
Light / Dark Mode

Full light-mode variant of the globe and admin panel for accessibility and presentation use in bright environments.

🎧
Ambient Audio

Optional cultural soundscapes per region — nature sounds, instruments, chants — activated when exploring specific culture nodes.

📖
Print Cards

Export any culture as a clean one-page PDF summary — ideal for educators, researchers, and community presentations.
